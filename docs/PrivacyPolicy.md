# Roost Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 23, 2026

Roost is a local-first macOS app for saving notes, links, files, screenshots, projects, and optional calendar context.

## Data Collection

Roost does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data to the developer or to third-party analytics services.

## Local Data

Roost stores your saved notes, bookmarks, projects, tags, and app preferences locally on your Mac in Application Support and UserDefaults. This data stays on your device unless you choose to back it up, share it, or move it using macOS or another service.

## Calendar Access

Roost can optionally request read-only access to your calendars to show today's events beside your notes. Calendar events are read locally and are not uploaded or written back.

## File Access

Roost can save references to files you drag, paste, or otherwise choose. For sandboxed Mac App Store builds, Roost stores security-scoped bookmark data when macOS grants file access so selected files can be reopened later.

## Network Access

Roost may use the network to fetch page titles for web links you save. Roost does not use this access for tracking, advertising, analytics, or profiling.

## Screenshots

Screenshot capture is optional. If enabled, Roost watches for new screenshots on your Mac and saves local references to them in the Screenshots collection. Screenshots are not uploaded by Roost.
